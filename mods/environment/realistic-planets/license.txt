Realistic Planets, a mod for OpenReliant
========================================

The textures (realistic_planets_*.png) and the thumbnail (mod.png) are made by OpenReliant: Neptune's
from NASA/JPL's Voyager 2 images and Jupiter's from NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute's Cassini map,
both in the public domain, Jupiter's calibrated with Hubble's OPAL maps (CC BY 4.0), Uranus's from
Hubble's OPAL maps, Saturn's and its ring's from Cassini's maps and pictures, Hubble's OPAL maps
and Voyager 2's measurements of the rings, Venus's from JAXA/ISAS's Akatsuki maps (CC BY 4.0),
and Titan's from NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute's Cassini pictures, in the public domain, and
NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona's Cassini map of its surface (PIA22770), free to use with
that credit.
They, and the note on how Neptune's is made (neptune-voyager-map.pdf), are shared under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): you may share and adapt them for any purpose,
crediting "OpenReliant, from NASA/JPL Voyager 2 data" for Neptune, "OpenReliant, from NASA/JPL/Space
Science Institute Cassini data and NASA/ESA Hubble OPAL data" for Jupiter, "OpenReliant, from
NASA/ESA Hubble OPAL data" for Uranus, "OpenReliant, from NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute Cassini
data, NASA/ESA Hubble OPAL data and NASA/JPL Voyager 2 data" for Saturn and its ring,
"OpenReliant, from JAXA/ISAS Akatsuki UVI data" for Venus, and "OpenReliant, from NASA/JPL/Space
Science Institute Cassini data and NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona's map" for Titan.

The spheres (*.shp), mod.ini and the scripts that make the mod's files are OpenReliant's, under the
Mozilla Public License 2.0.

README.md lists every source used and how each texture is made.
