Realistic Planets, a mod for OpenReliant
========================================

The textures (realistic_planets_*.png) and the thumbnail (mod.png) are made by OpenReliant: Neptune's
from NASA/JPL's Voyager 2 images and Jupiter's from NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute's Cassini map,
both in the public domain, Jupiter's calibrated with Hubble's OPAL maps, and Uranus's from Hubble's
OPAL maps (CC BY 4.0). They, and the note on how Neptune's is made (neptune-voyager-map.pdf), are
shared under CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/): you may share and adapt them
for any purpose, crediting "OpenReliant, from NASA/JPL Voyager 2 data" for Neptune, "OpenReliant,
from NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute Cassini data and NASA/ESA Hubble OPAL data" for Jupiter, and
"OpenReliant, from NASA/ESA Hubble OPAL data" for Uranus.

The spheres (*.shp), mod.ini and the scripts that make the mod's files are OpenReliant's, under the
Mozilla Public License 2.0.

README.md lists every source used and how each texture is made.
